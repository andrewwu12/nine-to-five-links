          How to play

          Aim
          Press and drag anywhere on the course to turn the putter toward that spot, or use the arrow keys (Shift for fine steps).
          Swing
          Hold Swing (or Space) to draw the putter back. The meter's marks show how far a putt rolls on a flat, medium green. Hold too long and it comes forward again. Let go to start the stroke, then tap again as the needle crosses the green zone. Tap early and the putt pushes right; tap late and it pulls left. Miss the tap entirely and you shank it. Esc cancels.
          Read the green
          Every green slopes a little, and the chevrons point downhill, so putts curve that way as they slow down. The swing meter also shows the green's speed: fast greens roll out further, slow ones come up short.
          Turns
          On one screen, each player finishes the hole, then hands over. Whoever scored best on the last hole tees off first.
          Online
          Choose Online room and send your coworkers the invite link. Everyone plays the same hole at the same time on their own device and sees each other's balls. The next hole starts when everyone has finished, or you can go on ahead.
          Scoring
          Seven strokes max per hole. Coffee spills, the shredder, the loading dock and the service pit cost a stroke, and you play again from where you hit.
          Rage mode
          Waxed floors, bouncy walls, a smaller cup, faster everything, shaky hands and no aim guide. A Roomba roams the course and bumps your ball, hazards send you back to the tee, and the elevator sometimes picks the wrong floor. Ten strokes max per hole.
          Taunts
          The office has opinions about your putting. Press Heckle to hear them, or in an online room press Taunt to send your coworkers a line. Turn taunts off in the menu.
          Buildings
          Head Office runs the day shift, 9 AM to 5 PM. The Factory Floor runs second shift, 3 PM to 11 PM, with forklifts, conveyor lines, robot arms, oil spills and a loading dock to fall off. It gets darker as the shift goes on.
          Knock things over
          Cones, cup pyramids, boxes, chairs, bins, water jugs and oil drums all move when you hit them. Hit a box, crate or water jug hard enough and it breaks. A burst jug or a tipped oil drum leaves the floor slippery.
          Course
          Pin positions change every day. Everyone on today's pins, or on the same group code, plays the same layout, so scores compare fairly across devices.
